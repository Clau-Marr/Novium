Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026

Novium ("the App") is a billing and payment-tracking tool built for independent freelance interpreters. This policy explains what information the App accesses, how it's stored, and how it's used.

The short version

Your business data — clients, assignments, invoices, and payment records — is stored securely in Novium's private cloud database, with automatic backup copies saved to your own Google Drive. Nobody else's data is mixed with yours, and nothing is sold or shared with third parties beyond the service providers described below.

Information you enter directly

When you use the App, you may enter information such as:

  • Client/agency names, contact details, and billing rates
  • Assignment details (dates, times, patient names, locations)
  • Invoice records and payment history
  • Your own business name and mailing address, for use on invoices

This information is saved securely in Novium's private cloud database, tied to your account.

Information accessed through Google

If you choose to connect your Google account, the App requests the following permissions, used only for the specific purposes below:

  • Google Calendar events (read-only): to read the events (title, date, and start and end time) on your primary calendar so the App can import them as billable assignments. The App never creates, edits, or deletes anything on your calendar. Assignment details imported from your calendar, including any patient names in event titles, are stored in your Novium account as described above.
  • Gmail (send-only): to let you send invoices as emails from your own Gmail account. Novium requests this permission only when you choose to email an invoice. It can send only messages you write and review in the App, and it cannot read, search, or delete any email.
  • Google Drive (per-file access): to save up to three rolling backup files in a folder named "Novium Backups" that the App creates in your Drive, so your data isn't lost if your browser storage is cleared. When a fourth backup is saved, the oldest is deleted. The App cannot see, access, or modify any other file in your Drive — only files it creates itself.
  • Basic profile (name and email address): to show which Google account is connected to the App.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Novium's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

We use information received from Google only to provide the features described above. We do not use it for advertising, and we do not sell it. No person at Novium reads your Google data unless you ask us to for support, it is needed for security or to investigate abuse, or the law requires it.

How we protect your data

Novium is built to keep your data secure using standard industry practices:

  • Encryption in transit. All connections between your browser and Novium's servers use HTTPS (TLS encryption). Your data is never transmitted in plain text.
  • Encryption at rest. Your data is stored in a managed PostgreSQL database (via Supabase) that encrypts data at rest.
  • Per-account data isolation. The database enforces Row Level Security, meaning the database itself, not just the App, restricts every request so an account can only ever read or write its own data. Other users' data is never accessible, even if the App had a bug.
  • Password security. Passwords are never stored by Novium in readable form. Authentication is handled by Supabase Auth, which stores only a securely hashed version of your password.
  • Google data handling. Data received from Google APIs is transmitted over encrypted connections and stored under the same protections described above. It is never transmitted to any third party except the service providers named in this policy (our database provider and, for payments, Stripe).
  • Access controls. Only you can access your account's data through the App. Novium staff do not access individual accounts except when necessary for support you've requested, to investigate a security issue, or as required by law.

Payment processing

Subscription payments are processed by Stripe, a third-party payment processor. When you subscribe, your payment card details are entered directly with Stripe and are never stored on or transmitted through Novium's own servers. Stripe uses this information solely to process your payment and manage your subscription, in accordance with Stripe's own privacy policy. Novium retains only the subscription status and billing history needed to manage your account (e.g., which plan you're on and whether payment is current) — not your card details.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell, rent, or share your data with advertisers or other third parties.
  • We don't use your data to train any AI model.
  • We don't use tracking cookies or third-party analytics.
  • We don't share your data with any service provider beyond what's needed to operate the App (currently: our cloud database provider, Google, for the integrations described above, and Stripe, for payment processing).

Data retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you cancel your subscription, your data remains accessible in a read-only state for 30 days, after which it may be permanently deleted unless you request otherwise. If you request deletion at any time (see below), we will delete your data within 30 days, except where a short retention period is required to comply with legal, tax, or accounting obligations.

Your control over your data

You can disconnect your Google account at any time from within the App, which also revokes the App's access at Google. You can also remove access at any time in your Google Account settings under "Third-party apps & services." You can download a full copy of your data at any time from within the App.

You may request deletion of your account and all associated data by emailing masprofessionalservices.office@gmail.com. Once we've verified the request, we will permanently delete your data from our systems within 30 days, except where a limited retention period is required for legal, tax, or accounting purposes. Deleted data cannot be recovered.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after a change means you accept the updated policy.
